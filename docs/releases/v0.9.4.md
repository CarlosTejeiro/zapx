## ZAPX 0.9.4 — In-terminal search, done right

### ✨ New

- **Search inside a tab (`Ctrl+F`) is now a real find bar.** Type a word and
  ZAPX:
  - **highlights every match** in the buffer and marks them on the scrollbar,
  - shows a **live match counter** (`3 / 12`),
  - offers **match-case**, **whole-word** and **regex** toggles,
  - steps with **Enter** (next) and **Shift+Enter** (previous),
  - turns the box **red** when nothing matches.

  `Esc` (or the 🔍 button) closes it.

### ⚠️ macOS: "ZAPX is damaged and can't be opened"

The macOS bundles are not signed/notarized. After dragging the app to
Applications, run `xattr -cr /Applications/ZAPX.app` — or right-click →
**Open** on first launch.
