## ZAPX 0.10.2 — Window controls fixes

### 🐛 Fixed

- **Maximize button did nothing** — the custom titlebar's maximize toggle was
  missing the required window permissions, so the request was silently denied.
  It now maximizes/restores as expected.
- **Couldn't resize from the corners** — the corner resize zones were only 8×8
  px, too small to grab (the top/bottom/side edges already worked). They're now
  16 px, and the min/max/close buttons stay clickable over the top-right corner.

### ⚠️ macOS: "ZAPX is damaged and can't be opened"

The macOS bundles are not signed/notarized. After dragging the app to
Applications, run `xattr -cr /Applications/ZAPX.app` — or right-click →
**Open** on first launch.
