## ZAPX 0.4.0 — Your sessions, everywhere

### ✨ New

- **Export / import your whole environment** — **File → Export sessions…**
  writes one JSON file with your sessions, folders, broadcast groups,
  snippets and highlight rules. Import it on another machine (or as a
  backup restore) and everything comes back: nested folders, ProxyJump
  bastion links, group memberships. Importing is safe to repeat — items
  that already exist are skipped and you get a summary of what changed.
- **Import from `~/.ssh/config`** — **File → Import from SSH config…**
  turns your OpenSSH hosts into ZAPX sessions in one click: HostName, User,
  Port, IdentityFile (key auth) and ProxyJump between hosts of the file,
  `Include` directives included. Wildcard/`Match` blocks are skipped with
  a warning.

### 🔐 Security

Passwords are **never** exported — they stay in your OS keyring. Imported
password sessions simply prompt on first connect. Login scripts do travel
verbatim (their `send` steps may contain secrets you typed), and the export
dialog warns about it.

### ⚠️ macOS: "ZAPX is damaged and can't be opened"

The macOS bundles are not yet signed/notarized. After dragging the app to
Applications, run `xattr -cr /Applications/ZAPX.app` — or right-click →
**Open** on first launch.
